About Student Portal

Student Portal is a simple and user-friendly web application designed to manage student information easily and efficiently.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an easy platform for managing student records. Users can add, view, and search student information quickly without confusion.

Key Features

  • View complete student list
  • Add new student records
  • Search students by name
  • View detailed student profile
  • Simple and clean user interface

Why This Website?

This website helps schools, colleges, and training centers maintain student data in an organized way. It is lightweight, fast, and very easy to use.
